Key Benefits

Why Choose MBBS Abroad?

Thousands of Indian students choose overseas medical education every year - here's why.

Affordable Fees

Total MBBS costs abroad are often a fraction of private college fees in India - starting from ₹14 lakhs.

Zero Donation

No capitation fees, no management quota charges. Pay only the official tuition fee directly to the university.

Global Recognition

NMC, WHO, and globally recognised degrees that allow you to practice medicine worldwide after clearing FMGE.

English Medium

All our partner countries offer MBBS in English - no language barriers for Indian students.

Clinical Exposure

Mandatory clinical rotations and internships ensure hands-on hospital training alongside theory.

Safe Campuses

Dedicated Indian student associations, hostel facilities, and supportive university environments.

FMGE / NEXT Support

Career Futures provides FMGE preparation guidance so you can return and practice in India with confidence.

End-to-End Guidance

From shortlisting to arrival - we handle documents, visa (where needed), flights and registration support.
