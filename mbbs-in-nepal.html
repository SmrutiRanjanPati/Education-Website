Key Advantages

Why Study MBBS in Nepal?

Nepal offers Indian students a unique combination of affordability, cultural familiarity, and global recognition.

No Visa Required

Indian students can travel and study in Nepal without any visa due to the open border treaty between the two countries.

Zero Donation

No capitation fees or donations required for admission. Pay only the official tuition fee with complete transparency.

Globally Recognised

Nepal's medical degrees are recognised by NMC, WHO, and medical councils across the world.

No IELTS / TOEFL

No English proficiency tests required. Classes are conducted entirely in English, making the transition seamless.

Similar Food Culture

Nepal's cuisine and culture are very similar to India's, making it easy for students to adjust quickly.

Mandatory Internship

Medical students must complete a clinical internship, ensuring hands-on experience before graduation.

Safe Environment

Nepal is known for its safe, friendly environment with rich multi-ethnic culture and traditional festivals.

Quality Education

Excellent medical education system with modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and well-equipped hospitals.

Requirements

Eligibility & Admission Process

Check the criteria and follow these steps to secure your MBBS seat in Nepal.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum age of 17 years at time of admission
  • Passed 10+2 with at least 50% marks in Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB)
  • Reserved category (SC/ST/OBC) requires minimum 40% in PCB
  • Valid NEET scorecard is mandatory for Indian students
  • No IELTS or TOEFL required for admission
  • Medical fitness certificate from a registered physician

Admission Procedure

  • Fill out the application form of your chosen university
  • Submit scanned copies of passport & academic documents
  • Receive the admission letter via post or online portal
  • Confirm your MBBS seat and begin immigration formalities
  • Collect your official offer letter from the university
  • Obtain recommendation letter from Ministry of Education, Nepal
