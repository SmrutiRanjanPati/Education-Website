Eligibility & Admission Process

Review the standard academic prerequisites required to lock in certified processing.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum age of 17 years completed by December 31st of intake year.
  • Passed 10+2 with a aggregate minimum of 50% marks across PCB core variants.
  • Reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC) require an updated minimum of 40% inside PCB.
  • A valid qualified NEET scorecard is absolutely mandatory.
  • Cleared medical fitness validation authenticated by a registered provider.

Admission Procedures

  • Deploy structured registration applications directly into university allocations portals.
  • Submit validated verified sets of academic records and identification criteria.
  • Secure explicit official allocation confirmation letters from your college.
  • Process seat allocation reservations via institutional accounts.
  • Acquire requisite recommendation clearances via the Ministry of Education, Nepal.
